Three more brands get the full Playbook free, including 90 days of Operator. In return we ask for honest feedback and permission to share what we learn, with your name or without. Apply above; we confirm fit within two business days.

Your Amazon and Walmart listings, your store, your ad accounts, and how you show up in AI shopping answers.
