The five pillars

Every reason a buyer says yes, or not yet.

01

Digital Shelf & PDP

Is your product page actually built to convert attention into a sale. We check image and content quality, review volume and rating, keyword coverage, and buy-box control.

02

Retail Media Readiness

Is your paid engine mature enough to point at retail, and pointed the right way. We check ad performance, creative fatigue, and hook diversity.

03

AI & Search Discoverability

Do you show up when a shopper, or an AI shopping assistant, asks the category question. We check structured product data and content clarity.

04

Marketplace & Velocity

Can your ops and fulfillment actually support the volume a retail bet requires. We check fulfillment model, reorder lead time, and stockout history.

05

Brand & Category Fit

Does your positioning read as ready to a buyer, and are you part of the current cultural conversation in your category. We check category whitespace, positioning clarity, and cultural relevance.
